Moonflare: Dragon Run - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026 · App: Moonflare: Dragon Run (Android package com.ashour.forestrunner) · Developer: Ashour

This policy explains what information Moonflare: Dragon Run ("the game", "we") uses, why, and the choices you have. The short version: we don't ask you to create an account, we don't collect your name, e-mail or contacts, and your game progress stays on your device. Our advertising partner (Unity Ads) and Google Play (for purchases) process some data as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

  • Game progress and settings - best distance, gems, keys, unlocked heroes, missions, rewards, sound and graphics settings. Stored locally on your device only. We do not upload it.
  • Profile name and picture - if you type a name or choose a picture, it is stored only on your device. If you pick your own photo, the game uses the Android photo picker to read only the one image you select, makes a small copy for your profile inside the app's private storage, and never uploads or shares it.

2. Advertising (Unity Ads)

The game shows optional reward videos provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). When an ad is requested, Unity Ads may collect and process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, language, and ad interaction data, to deliver ads, measure them, limit how often you see them, and prevent fraud.

  • Your choice: on first launch the game asks whether ads may be personalized. If you choose "No thanks", ads are still shown but are not personalized. You can change this at any time in Settings > Ads & privacy.
  • You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads / Google > Ads).
  • Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

3. In-app purchases

Purchases of virtual items (gems, keys, bundles) are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. To deliver what you bought, the game (through Unity In-App Purchasing) receives a purchase confirmation and order information from Google Play. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment: policies.google.com/privacy.

3b. iPhone and iPad

On iOS the game first shows Apple's "Allow tracking?" prompt (App Tracking Transparency). If you allow it, Unity Ads may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show personalized ads; if not, ads are not personalized. You can change this any time in the iOS Settings app > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or Settings > Moonflare. Purchases on iOS are made through the Apple App Store and are subject to Apple's terms; restore them with Settings > Restore purchases.

4. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, location, or photos (other than the one profile picture you may choose, which stays on your device).
  • The game works offline; an internet connection is only used for ads and purchases.

5. Children

Moonflare: Dragon Run is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your game data stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game. Data processed by Unity Ads and Google Play is kept according to their own policies; you can contact them, or us, to exercise your rights (access, correction, deletion, objection, withdrawing consent).

7. Your rights (EEA, UK, California and other regions)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time (Settings > Ads & privacy). To make a request, e-mail us at the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version; important changes will be announced in the game or store listing.
